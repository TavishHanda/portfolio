An ordering and staff management site for a local pizza shop, built for our database systems course. Customers sign up, order from the menu, and check out; employees and the owner each get their own view, and the owner sets weekly shift schedules. I worked mostly on the database side: the tables for customers, employees, orders, order items, menu, and schedules, and the parameterized queries the pages run against Azure SQL.
